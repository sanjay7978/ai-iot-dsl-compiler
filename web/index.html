          01How it works
          
Seven phases. Every one of them visible.

          
Most compilers are a black box. Kelvin records what each phase does and shows you the real data structures: the automaton, the grammar, the tree, the types, the three-address code and the optimizer's work.

        03AI, kept honest
        
The model proposes.
The compiler decides.

        
Language models are good at reading intent but bad at being exactly right. In Kelvin, nothing an AI writes reaches your editor until iotc has compiled it.

        
    
          
  • Free providers: Groq, Gemini or a local Ollama model, used in that order of configuration. A rule-based converter works with no AI at all.
    • 
          
  • Every answer is compiled. If it has errors, the compiler's messages go back to the model for one repair attempt.
    • 
          
  • An AI fix is offered only if the error it targets disappears and no new errors appear.
    •
